# OP-020 — Checkpoint preview and isolation-safe restore

**Status:** Proposed · **Priority:** P1 · **Effort:** L · **Origin:** T3 + Hermes
**Dependencies:** OP-019, OP-001; coordinate retention/backup inventory with OP-013.

## Problem and outcome
Outpost lacks an integrated coding checkpoint/restore product. Undo must preserve unrelated user work and clearly distinguish conversation rewind from filesystem restoration.

## Scope
Capture versioned checkpoints with task/run/worktree identity, base revision, included files, exclusions, and storage usage. Offer diff preview, selective restore, conversation-only rewind, and whole-worktree restore only when isolation is proven. Bind restore approval to both checkpoint and current workspace revision. Define quotas, retention, garbage collection, and behavior when checkpoint creation fails before a mutation.

## Safety and recovery
Refuse whole-tree restore for shared/overlapping workspaces, following T3's isolation rule. Never claim rollback reverses messages, deploys, or other external effects. Exclude secrets and oversized/generated artifacts under an explicit policy and show coverage gaps. Preserve hand edits through conflict detection rather than silent overwrite. Do not copy Hermes' opt-in/default choices without a product decision.

## Acceptance criteria
- Shared-workspace restore is refused while conversation-only rewind remains available.
- Preview and apply detect intervening edits and stale approval.
- Crash recovery cannot leave a partial restore reported as success.
- Retention respects active references; quota/exclusion behavior is visible and tested.
